The method

Research has a shape.
Delta finds it.

Built for the moment before the blank page, when a topic is too large to search in one move.

01

Break it down

Start with a research question and relevant context. Delta turns the topic into smaller questions that can actually be answered.

02

Sequence the search

Each question gets its own search terms and academic sources, ordered so background comes before the argument.

03

Build the case

Bring the findings back together with claims, disagreements, and limitations visible to the researcher.
